Demonstration audio was generated locally with Kokoro-82M v1.0.
Model: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0)
ONNX conversion and runtime: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx (MIT code)
The model weights and runtime are not distributed with this static application.
The 36 English cue recordings are placeholders, not Japanese accent conditions.
Four separate Japanese practice recordings use Kokoro voice jf_alpha and Misaki
Japanese G2P with the UniDic-lite dictionary. Their texts were newly authored.

Instruction reference:
Kang, O., Thomson, R. I., & Moran, M. (2018). Empirical approaches to measuring
the intelligibility of different varieties of English in predicting listener
comprehension. Language Learning, 68(1), 115-146. DOI: 10.1111/lang.12270.
The Japanese instructions are newly written; the reference's word scoring is
distinct from this application's mora alignment rules.
